The apps I wished I could find.

Each of these began the same way: I went looking on the App Store for something simple — a score pad for our Canasta evenings, a calculator that gives exact answers, a quick way to know whether I had taken my pills, a place for the family's recipes and the people they came from — and couldn't find quite what I wanted. Too many ads, too many accounts, too much in the way. So I built the ones I use every day myself, and I'm glad to share them freely.

  • CalMo

    Exact math, graphs and calculus — a calculator, equation solver and physics companion that gives exact answers, with 359 formulas in 28 books, each with its own sketch, the periodic table and materials in charts. On iPhone, iPad and Android.
