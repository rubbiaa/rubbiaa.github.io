A look inside

As easy as asking your grandmother

Photograph the handwritten card or the cookbook page: Nonnina reads it for you, on the phone.

Tell it as you would to a friend — “my grandmother's tiramisù uses 500 g mascarpone, five eggs…” — and it becomes a tidy recipe.

From whom? is part of every recipe: each person has a page, with their photo, a few words and all their recipes.

Your version, next to the original: less sugar, a splash of Marsala — the original is never overwritten.

Tradition: recipes from the great historical cookbooks — Artusi, Beauvilliers, Dorn, Haller and more — with the original words and a version for today's kitchen.

Send it to someone: a link with the recipe inside it, by Messages, WhatsApp or e-mail. They add it to their own.

Your recipes are yours

Your recipes stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. No account, no ads, no tracking. Privacy
